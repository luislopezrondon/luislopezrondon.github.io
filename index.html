About

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at USF, expected to graduate in 2030. I lead electronics and circuitry for IEEE at USF's EXO team, where I work on battery selection, power distribution, and emergency-stop design for an exoskeleton, and I teach PCB design to newer members. Outside the team, I design custom PCBs in Altium for my own projects. I'm looking for internships in embedded systems, hardware, or robotics.
