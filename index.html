01 / About

Building the electronics that make robots move safely.

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at USF, expected to graduate in May 2029. Since August 2026 I've been the Electrical Systems Lead for IEEE at USF's EXO team, where I lead an electrical team with three sub-teams and coordinate with the Controls team on a powered exoskeleton. My part is the power system: battery selection, power distribution, and emergency-stop design. I also teach PCB design to newer members.

Outside the team, I design custom PCBs for my own projects. My interests are hardware and embedded engineering, robotics, and analog design, and eventually RF. I'm looking for internships in embedded systems, hardware, or robotics.
