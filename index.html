About

I got into hardware by wiring flex sensors and servos on a breadboard, and I haven't stopped since. These days I design custom PCBs in Altium, work through power and safety systems for a 48V exoskeleton, and teach newer IEEE members the basics of PCB design. I'm most interested in embedded and robotics engineering, especially where hardware meets control systems.
