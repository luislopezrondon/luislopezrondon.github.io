Prototype

The prototype is a Teensy 4.0 with the PJRC audio shield (SGTL5000 codec). It takes USB audio and aux line-in, runs a 1024-point FFT, groups the result into 16 logarithmically spaced bands, and draws the spectrum on an ILI9341 TFT display. Log spacing matches human hearing better than linear spacing.

Debugging story

The FFT output failed until I traced the problem to the Audio Library's memory pool being too small (AudioMemory(12)). Swapping in a peak detector isolated the signal chain and ruled out wiring. Raising the pool fixed it, and I'm now profiling with AudioMemoryUsageMax() to size it properly instead of guessing.

Custom PCB revision

With one teammate, I'm designing a custom board around an STM32F411. I'm learning STM32CubeIDE for it and prototyping on a Nucleo-F411RE. The planned design has:

  • An SGTL5000 codec
  • USB audio plus line-in input
  • Line-out and RCA outputs
  • A wide SSD1322 256×64 OLED for a classic analyzer look

My goals are to learn analog circuit design, since I'm designing most of the analog front end and filtering myself, and to learn high-speed PCB design. The board is currently in schematic capture.
