Audio Spectrum Analyzer

Real-time FFT analysis of USB audio on a Teensy 4.0, split into frequency bands and drawn on a TFT display.

Version 1

V1 takes audio from a computer over USB. The Teensy 4.0 shows up as a USB audio device, runs a 1024-point FFT on the incoming stream using the PJRC Audio Library, groups the results into 8 frequency bands, and draws them on an ILI9341 TFT display over SPI.

V1 signal chain
InputUSB audio from PC
Analyze1024-point FFT
Group8 frequency bands
DisplayILI9341 TFT

Adding line-in audio

I'm now adding line-in so the analyzer can take an aux signal as well as USB. I'm prototyping it with the PJRC audio shield. The next version moves to a custom PCB, designed with a teammate, that keeps USB audio and adds line-in through an ADC circuit I'm designing myself. It also adds a DAC output to speakers so the system can play audio, not just display it. I'm evaluating an STM32 for that board.

Planned chain
InputLine-in + USB audio
ConvertCustom ADC
ProcessSTM32 (planned)
OutputCustom DAC → speakers
